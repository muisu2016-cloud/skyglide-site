Privacy Policy

Sky Glide · MAX SPACE CONSTRUCTION LTD · Effective 16 October 2026

Sky Glide works offline. We do not create accounts, show ads, or sell data. Device permissions are used only for in-app features (glider emblem, call-sign voice note, reminders, a soaring day over your terrain, flight cards). Media and coordinates are not uploaded to us.

Information we collect

Our servers do not receive personal data from the app. Sky Glide does not require registration and does not send analytics or advertising identifiers to third parties. The only data are what you create yourself and what stay on your device (progress, settings, emblem, call-sign recording, day code).

Information stored on your device

Flight progress, medals, settings and related files (for example an emblem and a short voice recording) are stored in the app container using system mechanisms (including UserDefaults and files in the app sandbox). Deleting Sky Glide removes that data. Flight cards you explicitly save to Photos remain in your library until you delete them there.

Device permissions

  • Camera. Only to photograph an emblem for the glider fin. The photo stays on device. If you deny access, that feature is unavailable; the game still works.
  • Microphone. Only to record a short call sign. The recording stays on device. If you deny access, recording is unavailable.
  • Photos (read). To pick an emblem from your library. Photos are not uploaded by us. If you deny access, library pick is unavailable.
  • Photos (add only). To save a flight-result card to your library when you tap the button. If you deny access, saving is unavailable.
  • Location (When In Use). Only to build an open soaring day over terrain near you. Coordinates are processed on device and are not sent anywhere. If you deny access, regular flights remain available without that option.
  • Notifications. Local soaring-day reminders that you enable yourself. We do not send remote pushes from a server. If you deny access, reminders are not scheduled.

Network requests and third parties

Core gameplay does not require a network. The app does not call our servers for accounts, ads or analytics. Background audio runs locally on the device. The cumulus:// URL scheme opens a day code inside the app and does not share data with third parties.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data. Sky Glide is not designed as a children’s service with separate data collection for users under 13; the app does not ask for age or profile players.

Your rights

You control permissions in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes data from its container. Remove Photos library items there if you saved flight cards. Contact us at the support address below for policy questions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
